# Code of conduct

Flow Canvas is a collaborative project. Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming across experience levels and backgrounds. Critique ideas and code, not people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and publication of private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, moderate discussions, or restrict participation to protect the community. Report concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Do not post personal details in a public issue. The maintainer will review reports confidentially as practical and respond with proportionate action.
